FINANCIAL STEWARDS • CHAPTER 9
Scene 1

What Does Partnership Mean?

Is partnership just “giving somebody money”?
It’s more like joining the mission with them!
Lesson 9 begins with Paul's gratitude to the Philippians for their “fellowship” in the Gospel. The lesson explains that word as partnership.
The point is participation: helping someone carry out work you believe matters.
“For your fellowship in the gospel from the first day until now.” Philippians 1:5
2
Bible Adventure

The Philippians Kept Supporting Paul

The Philippians did not support Paul only while he was physically with them. They sent help when he was elsewhere, including more than once.
“For even in Thessalonica ye sent once and again unto my necessity.” Philippians 4:16
3
Shared Mission

Helping Paul Meant Helping the Gospel Go

They weren't only helping Paul eat...
They were helping the Gospel reach new places!
Lesson 9 emphasizes that their support helped establish churches and carry the message beyond their own city.
That is the simplest child-friendly picture of missionary partnership.
4
Paul's Heart

“Not Because I Desire a Gift...”

Paul explicitly says that was not the heart of his message.
Lesson 9 interprets partnership as something that benefits the giver spiritually as well as helping the work itself.
“Not because I desire a gift: but I desire fruit that may abound to your account.” Philippians 4:17
5
A Famous Promise

“My God Shall Supply...”

Lesson 9 stresses that Philippians 4:19 is spoken in the context of people who had repeatedly helped Paul spread the Gospel.
“But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus.” Philippians 4:19
Chapter emphasis: partnership is not passive admiration. The Philippians actively shared resources with the mission they believed in.
6
Gift and Opportunity

A Gift Can Open a Door

Can generosity create opportunities?
Lesson 9 interprets Proverbs 18:16 as referring to a present or monetary gift that can make room and create access.
The source distinguishes a legitimate gift from a corrupt bribe.
“A man's gift maketh room for him, and bringeth him before great men.” Proverbs 18:16
7
Bible Adventure

The Queen of Sheba Visits Solomon

I heard about your wisdom. I came to see for myself.
Ask what you came to learn.
The Queen of Sheba traveled to Solomon with valuable gifts because she wanted access to his wisdom and prosperity.
Lesson 9 uses her story as an example of giving toward a source of wisdom and value.
“...thy wisdom and prosperity exceedeth the fame which I heard.” 1 Kings 10:7
8
Long-Term Value

She Wanted More Than a Quick Fix

Short-term only:
Use all resources to solve today's needs without gaining wisdom for tomorrow.
Long-term thinking:
Invest in learning, wisdom, and partnerships that can help you serve better for years.
That becomes the bridge to one of Lesson 9's memorable phrases.
9
Key Phrase

“Sow Where You Want to Go”

The source says if you feel called toward a field, find people already doing good work there and support them.
For kids: Partnership can mean helping someone who is already doing the kind of good work you hope to do someday.
10
Motive Still Matters

Partnership Is Not a Shortcut to Riches

Lesson 9 explicitly says partnership is not about giving merely to get.
The giver's attitude, the place of giving, and trust in God all remain important.
No formula: Lesson 9 itself says there is no simple prosperity formula because attitude, where one gives, and trust in God all matter.
11
Be Deliberate

On-Purpose Partnership

The lesson recommends regular, intentional giving rather than only occasional giving after everything else is spent.
Partnership means commitment: identify good work, support it intentionally, and stay connected to the mission.
12
The Big Idea

We Can Go Farther Together

The Philippians could not all travel with Paul, but through partnership they participated in work beyond their own city.
Chapter 9 in one sentence:
Partnership means deliberately joining yourself to good kingdom work through prayer, resources, support, and shared purpose—helping the mission go farther than you could go alone.
13
Steward Challenge
