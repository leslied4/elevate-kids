The Tithe
FINANCIAL STEWARDS • CHAPTER 7
Scene 1

What Is a Tithe?

So “tithe” just means a tenth?
Yes. The lesson defines a tithe as a tenth part and tithing as giving a tenth of income back to God.
Lesson 7 is mainly about the motive behind tithing.
2
New Testament Giving

Not Grudgingly. Not of Necessity.

The lesson rejects giving because of fear of punishment.
The New Testament emphasis in this lesson is willing, cheerful giving.
“Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.” 2 Corinthians 9:7
3
Why Give?

Giving Says “Thank You”

Lesson 7 says New Covenant giving is an expression of appreciation for what God has already done.
“Thanks be unto God for his unspeakable gift.” 2 Corinthians 9:15
Order matters: We do not give so God will love us. We give because He has already loved and given to us.
4
Bible Adventure

Abraham Tithed Before the Law

Abraham gave a tenth?
Yes—to Melchizedek, long before Moses’ Law.
The lesson points to Abraham as evidence that tithing existed as a biblical principle before the Law.
That is the author's own conclusion in the lesson.
5
Old and New Covenant

Malachi Is Not a Threat Club

Lesson 7 objects to using Malachi 3 as a threat against Christians.
Galatians 3 is used to explain the author's distinction between Law and grace.
“Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law...” Galatians 3:13
6
Seed Picture

Don't Eat All the Seed

The lesson compares money with seed. Consuming every bit leaves nothing to plant.
Tithing is presented as one way to discipline ourselves not to consume everything.
Important: The lesson says God still loves a person even if they “eat all the seed.” The issue is wisdom and stewardship, not earning God's love.
7
Heart Over Percentage

10% With Fear—or 5% With Joy?

Lesson 7 says an exact percentage without the right motive misses the point.
The author explicitly says someone beginning to give may start where they can give cheerfully and grow from there.
The lesson's goal: eventually reach the place of asking, “God, this is Your money. What do You want me to do with it?”
8
No Manipulation

God's Blessings Don't Have a Countdown Timer

Lesson 7 gives examples of fundraising pressure and calls this manipulation.
The chapter repeatedly rejects buying prosperity or forcing God to bless us.
9
Purpose in Your Heart

Think. Pray. Decide. Give Cheerfully.

This is the child-friendly process that best captures the lesson's New Testament emphasis.
“Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give...” 2 Corinthians 9:7
10
The Big Idea

Tithing as a Starting Place

Lesson 7 says tithing is a starting place, while also arguing that New Covenant giving can grow beyond a fixed percentage.
Chapter 7 in one sentence:
Tithing is not presented here as a fear-payment that earns God's love, but as a biblical starting point for cheerful, grateful, faith-filled giving that flows from the heart.
11
Steward Challenge

Talk, Think & Do

🧠 Think About It

1. What does “tithe” mean?
2. According to 2 Corinthians 9:7, how should a person give?
3. Why does Lesson 7 point to Abraham?
4. Why does the lesson reject fear-based use of Malachi 3?
5. What does the “seed” picture teach?
6. Why can motive matter more than the exact percentage?
7. What kinds of fundraising pressure does the lesson reject?

😊 Cheerful-Giver Challenge

If you receive allowance, a gift, or money for a small job, talk with a parent or church teacher about setting aside a portion to give. The goal is not fear or guilt. Give with gratitude and joy.

“God, thank You for giving to me. Help me give with a cheerful heart.”
